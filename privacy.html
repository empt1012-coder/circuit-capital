Circuit & Capital (“we”) publishes this site and the newsletter. This policy describes what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Log data: IP address, browser, pages viewed, referring URL — typical server and analytics logs.
  • Analytics: if Google Analytics is connected in js/config.js, page views may be measured after you accept cookies. Necessary-only skips that script.
  • Newsletter: email address you submit, and whether you open or click.
  • Contact forms: whatever you type, plus the time of submission.
  • Cookies: a local preference for this demo’s cookie banner; advertising and analytics cookies once those vendors are connected.

Advertising

This site is built to run third-party ads (for example Google AdSense). Ad networks may use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads, measure them, and limit how often you see the same creative. When AdSense is live, Google’s partner policy and ad settings apply. You can opt out of personalized ads via Google’s ad settings and industry tools such as aboutads.info.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you serve readers in the EEA or UK, you need a real consent management platform before turning on personalized ads. This starter only stores a local “accepted / necessary” flag. It is not a GDPR-grade CMP.

Retention

Analytics: vendor default. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. Contact mail: as long as needed to reply and keep a record of the request.

Your choices

Unsubscribe links in every email. Cookie banner on first visit. Email the desk to request deletion of a contact submission.

Children

This site is for a general business audience. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
