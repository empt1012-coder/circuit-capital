Risk exception register

Track an exception, its owner, and its expiry. Known-exploited bugs, MFA, backups, and EDR are marked not waivable.

Not legal, tax, insurance, or procurement advice. Inputs stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Exceptions for a CISA Known Exploited Vulnerability, MFA, backups, or EDR are marked Not waivable. A row is also flagged when it has no owner, no expiry, or no funded fix, and when the expiry is past or inside 30 days. The file import is read in this browser. It is not uploaded.
