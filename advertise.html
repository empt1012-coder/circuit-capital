Direct vs network

Network (AdSense, Media.net, Ezoic): fill remnant inventory after you are approved. Paste your publisher ID into js/config.js and set provider to adsense.

Direct: higher yield for B2B software, cloud, fintech, and recruiting. We recommend keeping at least one native unit and the newsletter for direct deals even after a network is live.

Requirements

  • No animation that hijacks scroll. No fake “download” buttons.
  • Sponsored content is labeled. It does not run as news.
  • We can refuse categories (gambling, get-rich schemes, malware-adjacent).
