Advertise

Reach operators, investors, and builders who read for the P&L, not the hype cycle.

Circuit & Capital is an independent business and technology publication run by Emilio Santos, founder and editor. Readers are finance leads, security and IT managers, founders and board members who use the briefings, guides and free tools to make budget and control decisions. This page sets out how advertising works on the site, which formats exist, what we accept and refuse, and how advertising is kept apart from editorial decisions.

Inventory is labeled Advertisement or Sponsored. Network units are served by Google AdSense once the site is approved. Direct sponsorships are sold separately and never run as news.

Ad and sponsor policy

  • Editorial independence. Advertisers and sponsors do not choose, review, edit or approve any briefing, guide or tool before or after publication. They cannot ask for a story to be written, changed, delayed or removed. Coverage decisions are made by the editor alone, under our editorial standards.
  • No paid coverage. We do not accept payment, gifts or free products in exchange for coverage, links or reviews. If a piece mentions a company that also advertises with us, the piece is held to the same sourcing rules as any other.
  • Clear labels. Every display unit carries the label “Advertisement.” Sponsored content carries the label “Sponsored” and the sponsor’s name at the top, uses a visibly different card, and is never placed in a news list or the RSS feed as if it were a briefing.
  • Disclosure. If a sponsor has a relationship with a piece, such as underwriting a newsletter issue, the issue says so at the top.
  • Third-party ads. Network ads are selected and served by Google and its partners, not by us. A network ad is not an endorsement. Google’s use of cookies for advertising, and how to opt out, is explained in our privacy policy.

Direct vs network

Network: display units served through Google AdSense after approval. We do not sell or guarantee specific network placements.

Direct: B2B software, cloud, fintech, and recruiting. Native units stay available for those deals. Direct campaigns are booked by email, with dates, placement, creative and price confirmed in writing before anything runs.

What we accept and refuse

  • No animation that hijacks scroll, no auto-playing audio, no pop-ups that block the article, and no fake “download” or “close” buttons.
  • Creative must say who the advertiser is and link to the advertiser’s own domain.
  • Claims in an ad must be the advertiser’s to make and must not imitate our headlines, logo or byline.
  • We refuse gambling, get-rich schemes, unregistered investment offers, crypto token promotions, weapons, adult content, and anything malware-adjacent or deceptive. We can refuse or remove any ad without giving a reason.
  • Ads must not collect data beyond what the ad network’s own policies allow.

Response times

Advertising enquiries are answered within two business days. Send the advertiser name, the dates you have in mind and the audience you want to reach to circuits50capital@circuits.fit with the subject Advertising, or use the contact form with the topic set to Advertising. To report a problem with an ad you saw on the site, such as a misleading or broken ad, include the page address and a screenshot if you can; we review those reports within one business day.
