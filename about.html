About the briefing

Circuit & Capital is an independent, ad-supported publication covering business and technology — how companies buy software, how capital prices it, and how policy changes the map.

We publish original analysis, not recycled press releases. Sample stories on this starter site are editorial models you can replace with your own reporting. Change the name, authors, and domain in js/config.js and the page copy.

How we make money

Display ads, native sponsorships, and a newsletter. Editorial and advertising are labeled separately. Sponsored units say “Sponsored.” We do not sell newsroom decisions.
