About Circuit & Capital

An independent business and technology briefing, written by one person.

Circuit & Capital publishes analysis and operator guides on how companies buy software, how capital prices it, and how policy changes the map. Figures that come from a survey, a filing, or a government notice are cited on the page.

Coverage

Software buying and renewals. Cloud and AI spend. Private credit and the software roll-up. Cyber insurance as it lands in the IT budget. Export controls and the suppliers caught in between. Compliance when it is sold as a product. Short guides for the people who have to close the ticket.

The writing is for the P&L, the questionnaire, and the board pack — not the launch blog.

Who it’s for

Chief financial officers, chief information security officers, founders, and operators. The briefing is written in US and UK English for readers who sign budgets, insurance packets, and product bets on either side of the Atlantic, and anywhere the same buying problems show up.

How the site is paid for

The morning wire is free. Labeled display units and labeled sponsorships are the commercial inventory. They do not run as news, and they are not on the homepage.

A sponsor does not pick the briefing, kill a piece, or write the dek.

Corrections

Email circuit50capital@circuits.fit or use the contact form. The article is corrected, and the change is noted at the bottom of the page.

Author

Every briefing and every guide on this site is written by Emilio Santos. There is no other byline, correspondent, or contributor.
