Password managers and backup tools for small teams (2026)

Password managers and backup tools for a 3-50 person team compared on price, SSO and Microsoft 365 backup, with prices checked on each vendor’s page November 10, 2026.

Edited by Emilio Santos November 10, 2026 8 min read

Who this is for

Owners, office managers and small MSPs choosing a password manager and a backup setup for a team of 3 to 50 people, mostly on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.

On this page

  1. The short answer
  2. Password managers, compared
  3. Backing up Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace
  4. Backing up computers and shared files
  5. What 12 people cost per year
  6. FAQ

Two purchases cover most of what a cyber insurer, a client questionnaire and a bad Tuesday will test at a small firm: a password manager with an admin console, and backups you have actually restored. This page compares the options we would shortlist for each, with prices read from each vendor’s own page.

Disclosure. Circuit & Capital is an affiliate of RoboForm, IDrive, pCloud and Nord Security. Links marked “Paid link” may earn us a commission at no cost to you. Non-affiliate options are listed on the same terms, and named where they win.

The short answer

  • Passwords, lowest cost with provisioning: Bitwarden Teams at $4 per user per month, or RoboForm Business at $3.33 with SSO and SCIM on its one business plan.
  • Passwords, most polished apps: 1Password, especially the Teams Starter Pack for up to 10 people.
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace backup: a third-party backup such as IDrive Cloud Applications Backup at $20 per seat per year, or Microsoft 365 Backup if you want to stay inside Microsoft and pay by the gigabyte.
  • Laptops and desktops: IDrive Team if you want one price for a set number of computers, or Backblaze Business Backup at $99 per computer per year for unlimited data.
  • Shared files with version history: pCloud Business, with the caveat that synced storage is not a backup on its own.

For a team, the deciding line is usually SSO and provisioning: you want a leaver removed from the vault when you disable them in Microsoft 365. Bitwarden and RoboForm both put that on their entry business plan, which is unusual at their price. 1Password puts it on Business; NordPass and Keeper on Enterprise.

For an owner or a solo freelancer, a personal plan is the first step. Bitwarden has a free plan and a $19.80-a-year Premium plan. Backing up Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace keep your service running; restoring a mailbox or a SharePoint library after a mistake, a departed employee or ransomware is a job you still have to plan for. Our shared responsibility checklist covers why. Two ways to do it:

  • IDrive Cloud Applications Backup: $20 per seat per year for Microsoft 365 (OneDrive, Outlook and Exchange, SharePoint, Teams and Groups) or Google Workspace (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts), with a 7-day free trial. IDrive’s Google Workspace page includes 10 TB of storage per seat; its Microsoft 365 page lists the $20 seat price and does not state a storage cap. IDrive says it backs up three times a day and restores at item level; Entra ID objects are $10 per seat per year. This is the one we would start with for a small Microsoft 365 tenant, because the price is per user and predictable: IDrive Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace backup. Paid link: we may earn a commission.
  • Microsoft 365 Backup (Microsoft’s own): pay-as-you-go at a list price of $0.15 per GiB per month of protected content, bought in the Microsoft 365 admin center. It keeps everything inside Microsoft and restores fast, but the bill grows with your data, including deleted and versioned items held for recovery. Microsoft publishes a pricing calculator.

Backing up computers and shared files

  • IDrive Team: 5 computers and 5 users with 5 TB for $9.99 a month on a yearly plan ($11.99 month to month), with larger tiers up to 500 computers. IDrive shows a first-year discount on yearly plans; standard pricing applies from year two. IDrive online backup. Paid link: we may earn a commission.
  • Backblaze Business Backup: $99 a year per computer, unlimited data, central admin, SSO via Google and Microsoft, and free mailed-drive restores. The simplest pick if each person has one laptop and a lot of data. Not an affiliate link: Backblaze pricing.
  • pCloud Business: encrypted shared cloud storage for teams, $9.99 per user per month with a 3-user minimum and 1 TB per user, activity logs, and file version history up to 180 days on the vendor’s page. Business Pro shows $14.98 per user with 2 TB per user as a new-account discount. It is a good place for the files people work on together, but a synced folder will also sync a deletion, so pair it with a backup. pCloud Business. Paid link: we may earn a commission.
  • Backup software that needs a bucket: if an MSP tool or a NAS writes to S3-compatible storage, IDrive e2 is one option: IDrive e2. Paid link: we may earn a commission. Check object lock (immutability) support for your tool before you rely on it for ransomware recovery.

Whatever you pick, CISA’s ransomware guide recommends keeping offline, encrypted backups and testing them regularly. A backup you have never restored from is a hope, not a control. Write down the date of your last test restore; insurers ask for it.

What 12 people cost per year

An example from the list prices above, for a 12-person firm on Microsoft 365 with one laptop each. It is not a quote.

  • Password manager: Bitwarden Teams 12 × $4 × 12 = $576. RoboForm Business 12 × $3.33 × 12 = $479.52. 1Password Business 12 × $8.99 × 12 = $1,294.56.
  • Microsoft 365 backup: IDrive 12 × $20 = $240. Microsoft 365 Backup depends on data size; at an assumed 50 GiB per user, 600 GiB × $0.15 × 12 = $1,080.
  • Laptop backup: Backblaze 12 × $99 = $1,188. 12 laptops need IDrive Team’s 25-computer tier, which is $49.99 a month on a yearly plan, or $599.88 a year at standard pricing.

The two jobs together run roughly $1,000 to $2,500 a year for 12 people. That is less than most firms spend on a single day of incident response.

FAQ

Is OneDrive or Google Drive already a backup?

Not by itself. Sync copies a deletion or an encrypted file as quickly as it copies a good one. Version history helps, but a separate backup with its own retention is what lets you restore after ransomware or a departed admin.

Why are affiliate products on this page?

Because they fit the job at a small-firm price, and the commission helps fund the site. Every option is judged on the same lines, and we list non-affiliate tools such as Bitwarden, 1Password, Backblaze and Microsoft 365 Backup where they are the better fit.

Do we need both a password manager and backups?

Yes. They answer different questions on an insurance form: who can get in, and how fast you can recover when someone does.
