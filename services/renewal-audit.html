Services

Renewal Audit

Send us your list of software renewals. In 5 business days you get a written report on what to cut, what to renegotiate and what to keep, with every notice deadline on a calendar.

Fixed price: $199 Up to 25 renewals Delivered in 5 business days

What you send

A CSV or spreadsheet with up to 25 SaaS or software renewals. For each one, include:

  • Vendor (or an anonymized label, see the data note below)
  • Annual cost
  • Renewal date
  • Notice period
  • Owner
  • Usage notes: seats bought versus used, overlap with other tools, anything you already know is a problem

The renewal tracker template lists the same columns if you are starting from nothing.

What you get

A written report, delivered within 5 business days of confirmed scope and payment, covering:

  • A recommendation for each renewal: cut, renegotiate or keep, with the reason in plain terms.
  • The notice deadline for each contract, worked out from the renewal date and notice period you give us.
  • Negotiation points for each renewal marked renegotiate: what to ask for and what to bring to the call.
  • A renewal calendar showing every notice deadline and renewal date in order.

Who does the work

Emilio Santos and the Circuit & Capital team do the review. We use AI tools to help sort, draft and check the work. A person reads every line of your file and every line of the report before it goes to you.

Scope

Included

  • Up to 25 renewals from one organization.
  • One written report and one renewal calendar.
  • One round of clarifying questions by email if your file is missing something.

Not included

  • Legal review of contracts. This is not legal advice. For contract terms that matter, ask your lawyer.
  • Talking to vendors or negotiating on your behalf.
  • Logging into your systems or pulling usage data for you. We work from the file you send.
  • More than 25 renewals. Ask us for a quote if you have more.
  • Ongoing tracking after the report is delivered.

The report is only as accurate as the data you send. If a notice period or date is wrong in your file, it will be wrong in the calendar, so check the contract.

Your data

  • You may anonymize vendor names (for example, "CRM A" and "CRM B"). Tell us the category of each tool so the advice still makes sense.
  • Your files are deleted after the report is delivered.
  • We do not share your file or report with anyone. See the privacy policy.

How to order

  1. Email circuits50capital@circuits.fit with the subject "Renewal Audit" and a line on how many renewals you have. You can attach the file now or after we reply.
  2. We confirm the scope with you by email.
  3. Payment is arranged after the scope is confirmed. The 5 business days start once payment is in.

Email to order, $199

FAQ

Can I do this myself?

Yes. The free software renewal scorecard and the SaaS renewal checklist cover the same method one contract at a time. The audit is for people who would rather hand over the list.

What if I have fewer than 25 renewals?

The price is the same, $199.
