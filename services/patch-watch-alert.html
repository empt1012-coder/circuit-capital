Patch Watch Alert

A short weekly email listing the security fixes that matter for small teams: Microsoft, Chrome, common SaaS, and due dates on CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with what to patch first.

$9 a monthOne short email a weekCancel any time by replying

Most weeks bring more security fixes than a small team can read. Patch Watch is a short, prioritized list of the ones that usually matter: Microsoft and Chrome updates, common SaaS products, and items CISA has added to its exploited-vulnerabilities catalog. It is a reading aid, not a scan of your network, and it does not replace vendor advisories.

What’s in each email

  • A short list of the fixes to take first, in order, written in plain English.
  • Who each item applies to, so you can skip what you do not run.
  • A link to the vendor advisory or catalog entry for every factual claim.
  • Federal due dates from CISA’s catalog, noted as a priority signal. Those dates bind federal civilian agencies; they are not a legal deadline for a private firm.

Checkout has an optional field for the products you run, and you can also reply with that list later. We keep it on file so later emails are easier to skim. The weekly email itself is the same list for every subscriber.

An example from a past week

The text below is an example, built from real items in the week of November 10, 2026. It is not a live alert, and the versions quoted are the ones published that week.

What to patch first

  1. Google Chrome: CISA added CVE-2026-125350, a use-after-free in Chromium’s XML component, on November 10, with a federal due date of November 24. Confirm Chrome shows 157.0.8103.50/.51 or later (the November 9 fix; a later November 11 build such as 157.0.8103.78/.79 also counts) and update Microsoft Edge or Opera if you use them.
  2. Microsoft’s November 2026 Security Updates, published November 10. Install the Windows and Office updates, including the Outlook fix for CVE-2026-124194, which CISA also added to the catalog that day, due November 24.
  3. If you run Adobe Commerce or Magento: CVE-2026-48356 had a federal due date of November 10. Confirm the Adobe fix is in.

Also that week. Google Brotli (CVE-2026-111497) was due November 10. VMware ESX (CVE-2026-41709) was due November 11. Barracuda Email Security Gateway and Ivanti Connect Secure items were due November 13.

Sources for this example: CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog; Microsoft’s November 2026 Security Updates; Google Chrome Releases, November 11, 2026; our November 10–13 patch calendar and Chrome 157 briefing.

How this differs from the free newsletter

The free weekly Circuit & Capital briefing is still free. It covers the week’s articles and tools, not only patches. Patch Watch is shorter, patch-only, and ordered by what to do first. You can take both, or only the free one.

How subscribing works

  1. Pay $9 for the first month by card through Stripe’s secure checkout. Checkout has an optional field for the products you run. Stripe emails you a receipt and renews the subscription each month at the same price until you cancel.

  3. You receive the next weekly Patch Watch after that confirmation. There is no long-term contract.
