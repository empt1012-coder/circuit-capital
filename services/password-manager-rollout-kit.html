Password Manager Rollout Kit

Everything a small team needs to move to a company password manager in two weeks: a day-by-day plan, admin and offboarding checklists, a staff email, a one-page policy and a tracker. Works with Bitwarden, 1Password, NordPass, Keeper or any other mainstream product.

One-time price: $19Instant downloadPDF, Word, Markdown, Excel, CSV

Picking a password manager is the easy part. Getting 5 to 50 people to actually use it, without a shared spreadsheet hanging around and without locking anyone out, is where rollouts stall. This kit is the checklist and the templates we would use to do it in ten working days, with one person spending about an hour a day.

Buy the kit, $19

What’s inside

  • A 14-page PDF rollout guide with nine sections:
    • Pick-a-manager checklist: must-haves, fit questions and a decision rule
    • Two-week rollout plan, by day, with a 30-day check
    • Admin setup checklist: MFA, SSO and provisioning, recovery codes, emergency access, policies
    • Staff announcement email template
    • Staff how-to one-pager
    • Shared-vault structure template
    • Offboarding checklist for leavers
    • One-page password policy template
    • FAQ for staff pushback (“Can my boss see my passwords?”)
  • Editable copies of every section in Word (.docx) and Markdown.
  • A rollout tracker in Excel (plan, staff status, vaults, offboarding sheets) and CSV.

Who it’s for

  • Owners, office managers and the one “IT person” at a company of 5 to 50.
  • Teams still sharing logins in a spreadsheet, a notes app or the browser.
  • Anyone who has bought seats and now has to get everyone to use them.

If you are still choosing a product, start with our free guide: How to choose a password manager for a small business.

Vendor-neutral

The kit works with any mainstream password manager. It names Bitwarden, 1Password, NordPass and Keeper as common choices and gives the same checklist for each. Bitwarden’s free and low-cost plans are named alongside the paid options.
