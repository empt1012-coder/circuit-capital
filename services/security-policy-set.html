Security Policy Set

Fill in a short intake form about how your company works. You get written security policies tailored to those answers, in Word and PDF: the core 5 for $99, or a set of 12 for $199.

$99 for 5 policies$199 for 12 policiesDelivered in 4 to 5 business days

Customers, insurers and auditors ask small companies for written security policies long before those companies have a security team. Downloading generic templates is free, but a template that describes controls you do not have is worse than no policy: it is a written promise you are breaking. This service writes policies that match what you actually do today, with clear notes where you may want to do more.

What’s included

Core 5, $99

  • Information security policy (the umbrella document)
  • Access control policy
  • Incident response policy
  • Acceptable use policy
  • Data classification policy

Delivered in 4 business days, with one round of revisions.

Set of 12, $199

The core 5, plus:

  • Risk management policy
  • Vendor and third-party management policy
  • Business continuity and disaster recovery policy
  • Change management policy
  • Encryption policy
  • Logging and monitoring policy
  • Patch and vulnerability management policy

Delivered in 5 business days, with two rounds of revisions.

Every policy comes in editable Word and as PDF, with an owner, a review date and a version table filled in, plus a one-page cover note listing any place where the policy describes something you told us you do not yet do, so you can decide whether to fix the gap or soften the wording.

What we need from you

  • The completed intake form, which we email after you order. It takes about 20 to 30 minutes and asks how many people you have, where data lives (which cloud and SaaS tools), how people log in (MFA, single sign-on, shared accounts), how devices are managed, how backups work, and who handles incidents.
  • Your company name and the names or roles of policy owners.
  • If a customer or insurer asked for these policies, the exact wording of their request, so the set matches what they asked for.
  • Any existing policies you want kept or replaced.

Turnaround

4 business days for the core 5 and 5 business days for the set of 12, starting when we have the completed intake form and payment. Revisions come back within 2 business days of your comments.

Scope

Included

  • Policies for one organization, tailored to the intake answers.
  • One revision round (core 5) or two (set of 12).

Not included

  • Implementing the controls the policies describe.
  • Procedures, runbooks or technical standards for specific systems.
  • Mapping to every control in a framework such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA. We can note where a policy relates to common framework areas, but that is not a compliance mapping.
  • Logging into your systems to check what you told us.

FAQ

Will these policies make us SOC 2 or ISO 27001 compliant?

No. Policies are one part of those programs. An auditor will test whether you follow them, which depends on your controls, not on the documents.

Can I start with 5 and add the rest later?

Yes. Email us when you are ready and we will confirm the price for the remaining 7 before any work starts.

Are these just templates?

They start from our own standard structure and are rewritten to match your answers. Where you tell us you do not do something, the policy says so or the cover note flags it.

Do you offer this to MSPs for their clients?

Yes. Each client organization is a separate order, and we can leave our name off the documents. Say so when you order.

Can I track exceptions to these policies?

Yes. The free risk exception register records each exception, its owner and expiry.
